About Me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT), Bangladesh. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), graduating with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00 and ranking fifth among 120 students. My academic work combines university teaching, undergraduate research supervision, and research in computer vision and artificial intelligence. I also mentor students in programming and problem-solving.

Research

My research examines computer vision, video understanding, and multimodal learning, with a focus on robust visual representations and efficient perception. I am seeking PhD research opportunities in computer vision.

Research Interests

  • Computer vision and image understanding
  • Video understanding and anomaly detection
  • Multimodal representation learning and Vision Transformers
  • Spatiotemporal modeling and human-centered visual intelligence
