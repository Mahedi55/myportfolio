About Me

Mahedi Hasan is a Lecturer of the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department at IUBAT- International University of Business, Agriculture and Technology. IUBAT is one of the most renowned Private universities in Bangladesh. He pursued his Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). He ranked fifth (5th) among 120 students academically in the Computer Science and Engineering department with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00 in the 2017-2018 session. He is also awarded the Dean's Award for academic results. He is actively involved in research, as well as conducting classes, supervising practicums, and supervising theses. His research fields include Computer Vision, Image Processing, and Machine Learning. He also works in Cybersecurity. He published several research papers. He has worked with Medical Imaging, Object detection, Classification, Segmentation, Federated Learning, Intrusion detection, and Audio Signal. He has expertise in Competitive Programming and problem-solving. He is continuing to problem-solve from his undergraduate journey and is currently mentoring competitive programmers at IUBAT. He has solved over 1000 problems in different online judges and participated in several national onsite and virtual contests. He has a strong grasp on several programming languages, such as., C, C++, Python, and Java. He has also completed his Industrial Internship from an internationally reputed software company named Selise Digital Platforms in Bangladesh. He has certifications in CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate), Deep Learning Specialization, and Computer Vision Courses.
