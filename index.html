About Me

Mahedi Hasan is a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT), Bangladesh. He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), graduating with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00 and ranking fifth among 120 students. His academic work combines university teaching, undergraduate research supervision, and research in computer vision and artificial intelligence. He also mentors students in programming and problem-solving.

Research

My research focuses on computer vision and visual intelligence, particularly video understanding, human activity analysis, and multimodal learning. I am interested in developing reliable learning systems that combine visual, temporal, and structured information for applications in public safety, healthcare, and intelligent monitoring.

Research Areas

  • Computer vision and image understanding
  • Video analytics and human activity recognition
  • Multimodal learning and vision transformers
  • Human-centered and trustworthy artificial intelligence
