Cross-Attention-Based Multimodal Human Fall Detection

Affiliation: Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) · Undergraduate thesis research · 2023–2024

Combined pose-estimated skeletal keypoints and visual features for fall detection. Motion-guided keyframe selection reduced redundant frames. Cross-attention fused visual and skeletal representations, while an LSTM modeled temporal relationships.

Pose estimationGraph Neural NetworksVision TransformersCross-attentionLSTM

Reported accuracy: 98%, 95%, and 90% across evaluated experiments.

Deep Learning Pipeline for Automated Comet-Assay Image Analysis

Affiliation: International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT) · Research contributor · 2025–2026

Developed a computer-vision pipeline for DNA-damage analysis in fluorescence microscopy images. An attention-guided U-Net segments DNA head and tail structures. Crop-based CNN classification and morphometric features support damage grading. Accepted in Journal of Digital Health.

Two-Stage Surveillance Video Anomaly Detection

Affiliation: International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT) · First author · IEEE ECCE 2025

Developed a two-stage framework for surveillance-video analysis, using a CNN to distinguish normal from anomalous video content and a Vision Transformer to classify anomaly categories. The work uses the UCF-Crime dataset and a keyframe-based processing strategy.

Human-in-the-Loop Facial Emotion Recognition

Affiliation: International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT) · First author · IEEE QPAIN 2025

Investigated a Vision Transformer-based facial emotion recognition framework with confidence-guided human feedback. The approach incorporates human review of uncertain predictions and model updates to improve recognition performance.

GAN-Based Transaction Anomaly Detection

Affiliation: International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT) · First author · IEEE QPAIN 2025

Studied synthetic data generation for transaction anomaly detection under severe class imbalance. A conditional Generative Adversarial Network was used to support model development and evaluation on credit-card transaction data.
