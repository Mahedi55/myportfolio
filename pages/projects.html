Selected Research Projects

Selected research and development work. Publication links are provided where an associated paper is available.

Multimodal Human Fall Detection

Research project · Computer Vision and Healthcare AI

Explores indoor and outdoor fall recognition by combining visual features with human skeletal information. The proposed research direction integrates Vision Transformers, pose-based representations, graph learning, and cross-attention to distinguish fall events from non-fall activities.

Methods: Video analysis, Vision Transformers, pose estimation, Graph Neural Networks, multimodal fusion.

Two-Stage Surveillance Video Anomaly Detection

Published research · IEEE ECCE 2025

Developed a two-stage framework for surveillance-video analysis, using a CNN to distinguish normal from anomalous video content and a Vision Transformer to classify anomaly categories. The work uses the UCF-Crime dataset and a keyframe-based processing strategy.

Methods: Keyframe extraction, CNN, Vision Transformer, video classification.

Human-in-the-Loop Facial Emotion Recognition

Published research · IEEE QPAIN 2025

Investigated a Vision Transformer-based facial emotion recognition framework with confidence-guided human feedback. The approach incorporates human review of uncertain predictions and model updates to improve recognition performance.

Methods: Facial expression recognition, Vision Transformer, confidence-based feedback, continual learning.

GAN-Based Transaction Anomaly Detection

Published research · IEEE QPAIN 2025

Studied synthetic data generation for transaction anomaly detection under severe class imbalance. A conditional Generative Adversarial Network was used to support model development and evaluation on credit-card transaction data.

Methods: Conditional GAN, synthetic data generation, imbalanced classification, anomaly detection.
