Research supervision · 2024–present

Undergraduate Thesis Supervision

Selected undergraduate research supervised in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IUBAT.

Multi-Architecture Comparative Framework for Bangla Sign Language Classification Using CNN and Transformer Models

2024–present

Student researchers: Nazifa Tabassum and Sadia Sultana Jese

Supervised a comparative evaluation of ResNet50, VGG16, MobileNetV2, and a partially fine-tuned ViT-Base-Patch16-224 across 36 Bangla letter classes. The ViT model achieved 99.54% validation accuracy.

Outcome: Published in IEEE QPAIN 2026 · Supervising author

Enhanced YOLOv8 with Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) for Real-Time Bangladeshi License Plate Detection

2024–present

Student researchers: Prime Biswajit Barua and Sabikun Nahar Lima

Guided integration of CBAM into YOLOv8 to address occlusion, variable lighting, and Bangla plate formats. The team prepared the Bangla LPDB-A dataset of 1,928 annotated images and evaluated attention variants. The YOLOv8+CBAM model achieved 98.9% mAP@50 at 32 FPS on an RTX 3080 GPU.

Outcome: Published in IEEE QPAIN 2026 · Supervising author

Bangla Image Captioning Using Vision Transformer and BanglaBERT with Transformer-Based Cross-Attention Hybrid Fusion

2025–present

Student researchers: Anonto Chandra Das and Ratna Biswas

Supervised a multimodal architecture combining BanglaBERT contrastive pre-alignment with a hybrid decoder that uses dual cross-attention over local ViT patch tokens and the global CLS token. Evaluation used 17,380 existing caption pairs and 3,900 student-collected, human-annotated pairs.

Reported metrics: BLEU-4 0.5498 · METEOR 0.6488 · ROUGE-L 0.7040 · CIDEr 1.8981

Outcome: Manuscript in preparation · Supervising author

Attention-Guided YOLOv11 for Object Detection in Unstructured Bangladeshi Traffic

2024–present

Student researchers: Airin Aktar and Sumaiya Islam

Supervised development of a CBAM-integrated YOLOv11m model for occlusion-heavy local traffic, including rickshaws and CNG vehicles. The custom dataset comprises 265,698 annotated instances across nine vehicle classes. The reported mAP improved from 75.1% to 75.7%, while inference time decreased from 5.0 ms to 4.7 ms per frame.

Status: Ongoing undergraduate research
